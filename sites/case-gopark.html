goPark
Parking App

Turning the daily frustration of urban parking into a seamless, sensor-driven experience — while quietly steering cities toward a car-free future.

Overview

Smart parking for smarter cities.

goPark is a mobile app concept that helps drivers find, navigate to, and pay for parking in real time — while gently nudging them toward more sustainable mobility choices.

The Challenge

Parking is broken. Cities are paying the price.

Urban parking is one of the most frustrating daily experiences — and the numbers back it up:

  • Drivers spend up to 20 hours per year searching for a parking spot
  • 30% of inner-city traffic is caused by drivers circling for parking
  • 68% of urban land is dedicated to parking infrastructure

Existing apps lack real-time data, have outdated interfaces, and do nothing to address the bigger picture: too many cars, too little space.

The Approach

Design Thinking — From problem to prototype

Using the full Design Thinking process — from user research and competitor analysis through to wireframing, high-fidelity prototyping, and structured usability testing — goPark was built around three key user groups: drivers, parking operators, and city administrators.

  • Real-time parking availability via IoT sensor integration
  • In-app navigation, booking, and automatic payment
  • A "Nudging" layer surfacing transit alternatives, environmental impact, and cost savings
  • Branding built on clarity and trust: Proxima Nova, a teal-dark palette, and a logo that doubles as a map pin and a smile
Outcome

Rated "Excellent" Across the Board.

After testing with six participants spanning different age groups and technical backgrounds, goPark scored in the top benchmark category across all six UEQ dimensions — including attractiveness (2.53), stimulation (2.46), and efficiency (2.04).

The concept proved not only technically viable today, but also a strategic tool for cities looking to gradually reduce car dependency through smart infrastructure and positive behavioral design.
