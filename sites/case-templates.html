Templates for Canva & Adobe

A library of branded templates for Canva and Adobe CC — empowering non-designers and designers to ship on-brand.

Overview

Letting marketers ship without bottlenecking design.

Marketing teams move fast. They need a social post in two hours, a recap reel by tomorrow, an event flyer this afternoon. Routing every request through a small design team creates a bottleneck — and slow turnaround pushes people to make off-brand assets in PowerPoint.

This project built a template library across three platforms: Canva (still graphics, social, recruiting), After Effects and Premiere Pro (animated banner, transitions, intros, motion graphics) and Photoshop (email and online banners). All of it locked to the Liebherr brand guidelines.

  • 50+ Canva templates with locked brand elements
  • 30 After Effects and Premiere Pro motion templates (.mogrt)
  • Quarterly content drop and onboarding video
The Challenge

Freedom without losing control.

Every template needed two things at once: enough flexibility that marketers actually used it (instead of starting from scratch), and enough lockdown that they couldn't accidentally drift off-brand. Too rigid → ignored. Too loose → chaos.

We split each template into "locked" zones (logo, palette, typography pairings) and "open" zones (imagery, body copy, captions). Canva's element-locking and Adobes' essential graphics did the heavy lifting.

The Approach

Lock the brand, free the content.

Every template was built around a hierarchy: the things that must never change (logo placement, primary and secondary headlines, brand color) were locked or driven by linked styles. The things that should change (imagery, copy, language) had clear hotspots and short instructions inside the template itself.

  • Embedded usage notes and layer naming conventions inside every template
  • Quarterly content drop based on team requests
  • Teams channel for template support
Outcome

On-brand, without the bottleneck.

Marketing teams can now create their own assets independently, while the brand stays consistent across every channel. The design team spends less time on routine requests and more on the work that needs them.

Independent doesn't mean on their own, though: we still provide guidance for less experienced users and lend a helping hand whenever it's needed.

The unglamorous truth of corporate design at scale: a good template is used again and again, long after it leaves the designer's hands.
