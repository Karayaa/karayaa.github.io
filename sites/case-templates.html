Templates for Canva & Adobe

A library of branded templates for Canva, After Effects and Premiere Pro — empowering non-designers to ship on-brand.

Overview

Letting marketers ship without bottlenecking design.

Marketing teams move fast. They need a social post in two hours, a recap reel by tomorrow, an event flyer this afternoon. Routing every request through a small design team creates a bottleneck — and slow turnaround pushes people to make off-brand assets in PowerPoint.

This project built a template library across three platforms: Canva (still graphics, social, presentations), After Effects (animated lower-thirds, intros, motion graphics) and Premiere Pro (video templates, captioning kits, reel formats). All of it locked to brand tokens.

  • 120+ Canva templates with locked brand elements
  • 30 After Effects motion templates (.mogrt)
  • Premiere Pro caption & reel kits
  • Quarterly content drop and onboarding video
The Challenge

Freedom without losing control.

Every template needed two things at once: enough flexibility that marketers actually used it (instead of starting from scratch), and enough lockdown that they couldn't accidentally drift off-brand. Too rigid → ignored. Too loose → chaos.

We split each template into "locked" zones (logo, palette, typography pairings) and "open" zones (imagery, body copy, captions). Canva's element-locking and After Effects' essential graphics did the heavy lifting.

The Approach

Lock the brand, free the content.

Every template was built around a hierarchy: the things that must never change (logo placement, type ramp, color tokens) were locked or driven by linked styles. The things that should change (imagery, copy, language) had clear hotspots and short instructions inside the template itself.

  • Onboarding video for each platform (≤90 seconds)
  • Embedded usage notes inside every template
  • Quarterly content drop based on team requests
  • Slack channel for template support

Outcome

3,400+ on-brand assets shipped.

Six months after rollout, marketing teams had produced over 3,400 assets using the templates — none of which routed through the design team. Brand audit scores held steady at 94% on-brand application, up from 71% before templates existed.

The unglamorous truth of corporate design at scale: leverage matters more than craft on any single artifact. A good template ships ten thousand times.
