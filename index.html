01 — About
Hi, that's me

Five years of designing systems — from construction sites to consumer apps.

I'm Alex — a designer who lives between UX/UI and corporate identity. I started out in HMI design for industrial machines and now work across the full spectrum: design systems, mobile products, brand rollouts, and the templates that keep teams shipping.

I care about the unglamorous parts: variables architecture, naming conventions, component specs, and handover documentation. Good design is what survives after you leave the room.
